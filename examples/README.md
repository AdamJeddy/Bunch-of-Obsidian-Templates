# Completed examples

These are fictional notes with filled-in properties and working links. They are examples to explore, rather than templates to insert.

Open this `examples` folder as an Obsidian vault, or copy the `Project workflow` and `People` folders together into an existing vault. No community plugins are required. Keep them outside your vault's template folder.

Start with [Project Dashboard](Project%20workflow/Project%20Dashboard.md), then follow the wiki links in Obsidian. On GitHub, you can open the individual files here:
