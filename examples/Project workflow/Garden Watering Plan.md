# Garden Watering Plan

## Context

The beds need reliable watering while we learn how much care they require.

## Options

| Option | Benefits | Costs / risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volunteer rota | Easy to adjust; little setup | Needs reliable cover |
| Automatic watering | Consistent schedule | Equipment and setup before needs are clear |

## Decision and reasoning

Use a volunteer rota for the first month. Review reliability before buying equipment.

## Consequences

- Keep a backup volunteer for each day.
- Revisit if two watering sessions are missed.
