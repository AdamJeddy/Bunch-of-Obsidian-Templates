# Bunch of Obsidian Templates

> A curated collection of [Obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md) templates for personal knowledge management, productivity, and life organization.

---

## What is Obsidian?

The human brain is non-linear: we jump from idea to idea, all the time. Your second brain should work the same.

In Obsidian making and following connections is frictionless. Tend to your notes like a gardener; at the end of the day, sit back and marvel at your own knowledge graph.

Official Obsidian Website - https://obsidian.md

---

## About This Project

This is a collection of Obsidian templates I've made and curated over time. They're not designed to be perfect out of the box — instead, use them as a **starting point** to build your own workflow. Pick ideas, remix sections, and adapt them to your needs.

All templates use **Templater**-style variables (`{{date}}`, `{{title}}`, etc.). I recommend the [Templater](https://github.com/SilentVoid13/Templater) plugin for maximum flexibility.

---

## Usage

1. **Clone or download** this repository
2. Copy the template files into your Obsidian vault's templates folder
3. Set up [Templater](https://github.com/SilentVoid13/Templater) (or the core Templates plugin) to point to that folder
4. Create new notes from templates via the Templater insert command

> **Tip:** For Dataview queries to work, install the [Dataview](https://github.com/blacksmithgu/obsidian-dataview) plugin. All templates include Dataview compatible frontmatter fields.
